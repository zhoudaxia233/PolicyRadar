# Editorial review

The previous wording named a contribution ceiling without first explaining that
it caps the gross income used to calculate premiums. This was ambiguous between
income and the amount paid.

The revised title, summary, before/after, scope and limits explain the operation
using annual gross pay of EUR 80,000. EUR 69,750 and EUR 76,500 are income amounts;
EUR 6,750 is the additional pay counted, not additional premiums. Pension and
unemployment insurance are separate. EUR 84,150 is separately explained as an
eligibility threshold. No universal net-pay loss is asserted.

Chinese, German and English were reviewed for these distinctions and for the
unchanged pending Bundesrat consent. The official title and source URLs remain.
The old timeline event and intake row remain immutable with superseding display
text. This is an editorial clarification, not a new policy decision or a new
substantive factual review. Existing verification and coverage dates are retained.
