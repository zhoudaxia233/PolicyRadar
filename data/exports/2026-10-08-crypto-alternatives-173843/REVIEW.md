# Crypto holding-period correction — 8 October 2026

## Reader question and failure

Does the one-year private-sale relief still apply, and does rejection of Green
bill 21/5752 end efforts to change it? The previous explanation correctly recorded
the vote on that instrument but failed to follow the debate's explicit reference
to a separate BMF draft. The discovery registry also lacked BMF's legislative
directory. A correct isolated stage was therefore an incomplete answer to the
reader's question. Translation coverage and schema validation did not catch that
omission.

## Editorial and relationship review

Chinese, German and English titles, summaries, before/after, examples, limitations,
source notes and rules were reviewed together. Each policy explanation independently
states current law and the alternative instrument. The 2026 purchase / 2027 sale
example distinguishes the two acquisition cutoffs; the 2027 purchase / 2028 sale
example is explicitly conditional on enactment without material amendments.
The 25% figure is identified as a tax rate on capital income, not the sale price.
NFTs, security tokens, e-money tokens, business activity and staking/lending income
are not collapsed into a universal exemption claim.

The neutral topic groups the concrete holding-period reform question, while each
child retains its own sponsor, official identity, legal stage and dates. CARF and
DAC8 information-reporting obligations remain distinct from this tax-liability
question. The broader tax-vote discovery retains its original unverified intake
stage: verification of the crypto part does not verify every proposal in that report.
The 30 September BMF event records only that day's publication/procedure; the later
SPD speech is discussed in the current explanation, not backdated into that event.
