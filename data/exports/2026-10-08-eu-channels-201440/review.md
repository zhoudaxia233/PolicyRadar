## Repair directive: current law, old contracts and the October correction

Sources: Directive (EU) 2024/1799, https://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1799/oj ; 7 October corrigendum OJ:L_202690848, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202690848 ; current Commission repair-directive explanation.

- Article 5(1)–(7), Annex II: manufacturer duty follows listed EU reparability requirements, with impossibility and scope exceptions. Repairs outside the seller's guarantee can have a reasonable price; Article 16's seller remedies for non-conformity must be free. The prior generic phrase “repairs are not necessarily free” needed this distinction.
- Article 16, amended Article 10(2a), (3), (5a): one 12-month extension; longer national periods remain possible, and the specified no-fixed-period systems can provide equivalent protection of at least three years. It is not an unlimited extension after every repair.
- Article 21: Article 16 does not apply to sales contracts concluded before 31 July 2026. Both languages now state the acquisition/contract boundary. The dated hypothetical contrasts 1 July and 1 August 2026, without asserting national implementation or a universal retail warranty.
- Articles 7(2)–(3) and 22: July 2026 transposition/application is distinct from the 31 July 2027 common-interface/national-platform deadline. The explanation now retains the latter next step and phased status.
- October corrigendum: Article 18's added entry number changes from 29 to 30 only. It does not change the substantive consumer rights or dates. The directive and its official correction are grouped; the original event/revision history is preserved.
- Alternative paths: the law's references to Directive 2019/771 and national longer/equivalent guarantees were followed within its operative amendment text. National implementation and subsequent country-specific remedies remain outside this EU-only review and are explicitly unresolved. A correction to this EU explanation is not a claim of complete national legal review.

## Pig and poultry operating conditions

Sources: C(2026)6899 final, signed 8 October, Articles 1–5 and recitals 4–11; its annex; SWD(2026)298 final, sections 1–2. Download links are in the explanation and source ledger.

The decision implements the Chapter VIa / Annex Ia livestock regime of Directive 2010/75/EU as amended in 2024. This is an adopted Commission implementing decision, not a newly proposed general ban on farming. Existing rules continue through the transition. Article 4 specifies “not later than” four/five/six years from 8 October 2026. The card therefore uses no fabricated commencement date and displays the first implementation deadline, 8 October 2030. The bands at 600 and 400 livestock units are implementation bands, not universal scope thresholds and not animal headcounts. The 600-unit example explains that distinction. Annex Ia and its exclusions remain the scope authority; no claim of country-by-country implementation is made. Article 2 equivalent techniques and Article 3 alternative ammonia approaches require safeguards, not a blanket exemption. The source-linked decision, annex, staff assessment and announcement form one matter. Exact product/animal-specific numerical emission limits are not generalised into the summary.
