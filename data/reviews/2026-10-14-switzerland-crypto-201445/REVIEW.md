### `ch-crypto-aeoi-activation` (pending)

- **Current position:** the amended AIAG (AS 2026 48, amendment of 26 Sep 2025) took effect on 1 Jan 2026.
  - On 26 Nov 2025 the Federal Council decided that the CARF provisions of the AIAG and the AIAV do not apply in 2026 (SIF release).
  - The SIF FAQ of 18 May 2026 says there are no obligations in 2026 and implementation is 2027 at the earliest. The cut-off date for existing users moves to the day before the first activation.
- **Business 25.052 (partner states):**
  - The Council of States adopted both decrees on 10 Sep 2025.
  - The WAK-N suspended the business on 3 Nov 2025.
  - The National Council voted on 28 Sep 2026 (Herbstsession 2026). For decree 2 (review mechanism), entry passed 126:62 and adoption 126:61; both vote protocols are archived as PDFs.
- **Read but not archivable:** the parliament OData transcript for subject 82942. The Curia Vista and Official Bulletin pages render by script, so they could not be archived. The transcript records:
  - decree 1 (partner list) was not entered into, without a recorded vote, on the commission's unanimous proposal;
  - rapporteur Amoos and Federal Councillor Keller-Sutter said decree 1 is obsolete because the AIAG amendment in force since 1 Jan 2026 delegates the designation of partner states to the Federal Council; the business is to be written off once the Council of States follows;
  - Keller-Sutter said the Federal Council will decide on activation with the relevant partner states by the end of the year.

  Because the reader-facing explanation must cite archived evidence, it does not state the delegation, the non-entry on decree 1 or the year-end decision. Its limits say that who decides on activation, and when, was not verified from an archivable text. The intake row for 25.052 records these points as an intake note.
- **Source tension:** the May 2026 SIF FAQ still says the decision on activation "obliegt dem Parlament". The September debate says the power now lies with the Federal Council. The explanation states neither as fact.
