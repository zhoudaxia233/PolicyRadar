## Source-located review

### Promulgated Acts (BGBl. 2026 I Nos. 318–322)

- **No. 318 Federal Police Act** — new explanation. Read: Art. 1 §§ 29, 31a, 31b, 32, 39, 40, 60, 106; Art. 15 (in force 28 October). The previous 1994 Act was not compared; the explanation says so and does not claim any power is new.
- **No. 319 Science Freedom Act** — Art. 1 (§ 2 scope with 15 institutions and paragraph 2; § 4 EU-funding exception), Art. 2 (day after promulgation → 28 October). Limits now state precisely that paragraph 2 extends only § 4 to non-profit institutions not institutionally funded by the Bund.
- **No. 320 Asset recovery** — Art. 11: 28 October; Art. 9 (BaFin Act, technical) 20 November.
- **No. 321 Product liability** — Art. 1 §§ 1, 2, 16, 17, 23; Art. 17: new Act 9 December 2026; § 23 keeps the 1989 Act for products placed on the market by 8 December 2026. Title and impact rewritten; the earlier "subject to the published text" caveat is resolved.
- **No. 322 Design law** — Art. 7: 9 December 2027; Art. 5 (UWG § 15b) retroactive from 27 September 2026, Art. 6 deletes it on 27 September 2028. The transitional rule is now described in the limits.
- Each promulgated record is linked to its existing explanation by a new topic group; the explanations keep their parliamentary identifiers.
