Statutory Rules of Northern Ireland

2026 No. 1

Fisheries

The Shellfish Gathering (Conservation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

6th January 2026

Coming into operation

6th February 2026

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs(1) makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 124(1) and (2) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966(2) and now vested in it.

Citation and commencement

1.  These Regulations may be cited as the Shellfish Gathering (Conservation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and come into operation on 6th February 2026.

Interpretation

2.  In these Regulations—

“inter-tidal area” means the area between the high water mark of ordinary spring tides and the low water mark of ordinary spring tides;

“periwinkles” means the species Littorina littorea;

Prohibition on the taking of periwinkles

3.  Subject to regulation 4, a person must not take periwinkles by any means from the inter-tidal area during the period 1st January to 30th April, both dates inclusive, in any year.

Exemptions

4.  The prohibition imposed by regulation 3 does not apply to any person operating under the authority of, and in accordance with a permit granted under section 14 of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 6th January 2026.

Owen Lyttle

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prohibit the taking of periwinkles from the inter-tidal area in Northern Ireland during the period 1 January to 30 April each year.

An exemption to this prohibition applies where a person has been granted a permit by the Department under section 14 of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966.
