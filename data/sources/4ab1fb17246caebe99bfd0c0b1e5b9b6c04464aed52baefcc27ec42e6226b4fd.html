    Office of the Governor

    Sarah Huckabee Sanders is the 47th Governor of Arkansas and the first woman to serve as governor of the state.

    Lt. Governor

    Presiding over the state Senate and serving as governor if the governor is otherwise unable to discharge the office’s duties.

    Secretary of State

    Oversight of businesses transacting in the state, elections compliance & finance, and capitol facilities management.

    Attorney General

    The chief law enforcement officer of the state serves as legal representation for the state and its agencies.

    Treasurer of State

    Serving as the State’s banker  and responsible for an investment portfolio of about $9 billion.

    Auditor of State

    The state’s general accountant and payroll officer for the Executive, Legislative and Judicial branches.

    Commissioner of State Lands

    Overseeing the disposition of tax-delinquent property, historic preservation, and leasing of natural resources.

The Natural State

Arkansas is known for its picturesque landscapes and stunning natural beauty. The state boasts of an abundance of outdoor activities that can be enjoyed throughout the year, such as hiking, camping, fishing, and boating. The state is home to the Ozark and Ouachita Mountains, which offer breathtaking views, cascading waterfalls, and numerous hiking trails. Additionally, the state has over 600,000 acres of lakes and nearly 10,000 miles of rivers, making it a haven for fishing and water sports enthusiasts. With its mild climate, Arkansas is an ideal destination for outdoor enthusiasts looking to escape the hustle and bustle of city life and immerse themselves in nature.
