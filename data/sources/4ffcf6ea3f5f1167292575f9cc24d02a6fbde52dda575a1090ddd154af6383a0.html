Title

Reasons for repeal

1.

Customs

Council Regulation (EC) No 3036/94 of 8 December 1994 establishing economic outward processing arrangements applicable to certain textiles and clothing products reimported into the Community after working or processing in certain third countries

The regulation provided for one of the customs special procedures (outward processing) for textiles although by then, the special procedures were governed by the Community Customs Code. Since its establishment in 1994, the regulation has never been practically used and the required committee has never been set up. Outward processing is currently covered in the Union Customs Code.
