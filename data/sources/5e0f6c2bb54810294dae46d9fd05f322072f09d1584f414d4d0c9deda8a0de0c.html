    Sixth Session of the Intergovernmental Negotiating Committee on the United Nations Framework Convention on International Tax Cooperation

    Venue: United Nations Office in Nairobi (UNON)

    Date: 30 November to 10 December 2026

    Registration

    This registration portal is for:

    • Representatives of participating Member States;
    • Intergovernmental organizations and entities that have observer status with the General Assembly (GA);
    • Non-governmental organizations enjoying consultative status with the Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC);
    • All organizations formally granted accreditation to INC Tax as of 3 August 2026, available at the bottom of this page and on the Committee website;
    • All organizations which are included in draft decision A/AC.298/CRP.36, available at the bottom of this page and on the Committee website; and
    • Relevant organizations and bodies of the United Nations system.

     

    Registration for the Sixth Session opens from Saturday, 10 October 2026 and will close on Monday, 9 November 2026 (11:59 PM EDT).

    Note

    • Please be sure to check your spam folder periodically for any email communication from the Indico platform, if you cannot retrieve it in your inbox.
    • Registration does not guarantee participation. A receipt email will be sent to your inbox when registration is submitted. Upon approval by the Secretariat, an approval letter with a QR code and instructions on how to pick up your badge will be sent to your email. This QR code must be presented to security at UNON to receive a badge. 

     

    Technical support: For technical issues related to Indico account creation and registration, please contact the Indico Support Team at support.accreditation@un.org

    For other inquiries related to INC Tax, please contact the Secretariat at inc-tax@un.org
