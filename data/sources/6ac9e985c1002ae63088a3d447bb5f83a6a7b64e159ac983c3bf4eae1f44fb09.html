COUNCIL DECISION (CFSP) 2026/2397

of 26 October 2026

amending Decision 2010/573/CFSP concerning restrictive measures against the leadership of the Transnistrian region of the Republic of Moldova

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 29 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1)

On 27 September 2010, the Council adopted Decision 2010/573/CFSP (1).

(2)

On the basis of a review of Decision 2010/573/CFSP, the restrictive measures set out therein should be extended until 31 October 2027.

(3)

Decision 2010/573/CFSP should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

Article 1

In Article 4(2) of Decision 2010/573/CFSP, the date ‘31 October 2026’ is replaced by that of ‘31 October 2027’.

Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 October 2026.

For the Council

The President

M. HEYDON
