Nebraska State Officials

  • Jim Pillen

    Governor

    Jim Pillen is the 41st Governor of Nebraska, sworn into office in January 2023. A lifelong Nebraskan, veterinarian, and farmer, he is focused on cutting taxes, strengthening agriculture, and investing in education.

  • Joe Kelly

    Lieutenant Governor

    Joe Kelly serves as Nebraska's Lieutenant Governor. A former United States Attorney for the District of Nebraska, he brings decades of public service and legal leadership to the office.
