Public Notice 2026 – Negotiation for Lease

The Hawaiʻi Department of Agriculture & Biosecurity is accepting applications to qualify for agricultural leases on the islands of Hawaiʻi, Maui, and Oʻahu.

Application deadline: November 23, 2026, at 3:00 PM HST

How to Apply

Interested applicants must review the Public Notice and complete the Application and Qualification Questionnaire, including all required supporting attachments.

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements outlined in the Public Notice, including Hawaiʻi residency requirements and qualifications as a bona fide farmer or new farmer.

Submission Requirements:

  • One (1) original completed application
  • One (1) copy of the application
  • All required supporting documents and attachments

Completed application packages must be submitted to the Agricultural Resource Management Division (ARMD) at either:

  • Honolulu: 1428 South King Street, Honolulu, HI 96814
  • Hilo: 16 E. Lanikaula Street, Hilo, HI 96720

Deadline: November 23, 2026, at 3:00 PM HST

Late or incomplete submissions will not qualify applicants to submit a lease proposal. Qualified applicants will be notified in writing and invited to submit a lease proposal.

Please review the full Public Notice for complete eligibility requirements, application procedures, and additional information.
