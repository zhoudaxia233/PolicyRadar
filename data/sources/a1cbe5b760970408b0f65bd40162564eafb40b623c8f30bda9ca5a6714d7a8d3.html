Automatic translation disclaimer

The State of North Dakota provides automatic translation for nd.gov websites, courtesy of Google Translate. Please remember that the English language version is considered the most accurate. In the event of a disagreement or discrepancy between the translation and the original English version of this website or any notice or disclaimer, the original version will prevail.

Making North Dakota a better place to live, work, and raise a family

BE LEGENDARY

Welcome to North Dakota where possibility is as endless as the horizon.

Whether you crave the rugged outdoors or thrive in urban settings, you’ll find vibrant, diverse communities and the shortest average commute time in the country, leaving you with even more time to do something meaningful and unexpected.

We invite you to look around and see what North Dakota has to offer.  Imagine yourself living and working here. Know that in North Dakota, you can make a great life and a big difference.
