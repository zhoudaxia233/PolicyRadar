29 OCTOBER 2026

The EBA advises on targeted simplifications to Taxonomy disclosures

The European Banking Authority (EBA) has published its response to the European Commission’s Call for Technical Advice on certain key performance indicators (KPIs) and other aspects of the Disclosures Delegated Act under Article 8 of the Taxonomy Regulation. The EBA advises on targeted changes to streamline disclosure requirements, reduce complexity and enhance the usability of information disclosed by credit institutions and investment firms.

23 OCTOBER 2026

​The EBA appoints Kristīne Černaja-Mežmale as its new Vice-Chair

​The Board of Supervisors of the European Banking Authority (EBA) has elected Kristīne Černaja-Mežmale as its Vice-Chair for a duration of two and a half years. Ms Černaja-Mežmale will take up her new role on 1 November 2026 when the current EBA Vice-Chair, Helmut Ettl starts his new role as Executive Director of the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

22 OCTOBER 2026

The ​EBA extends some consultation deadlines due to website maintenance​

On 21 October 2026, CERT-EU, the cybersecurity service for the EU institutions, bodies and agencies, alerted organisations to two critical security flaws in software used to run many websites, including that of the European Banking Authority (EBA). As a precaution, the EBA took its website offline on Wednesday 21 October 2026 at 13:00 CEST while the necessary security updates were applied. The website was restored on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 14:00 CEST.

14 OCTOBER 2026

​The EBA classifies USDC as the first significant e-money token in the European Union

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) has classified USDC, issued by Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, as a significant e-money token (EMT) under the Markets in Crypto Assets Regulation (MiCA), following its assessment of the criteria set out in the Regulation. There are currently 45 EMTs in the European Union, which have been notified under MiCA, and USDC is the first one to be classified as being significant.

13 OCTOBER 2026

European Supervisory Authorities set priorities for 2027

The Joint Committee of the European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA and ESMA – the ESAs) published today its Annual Work Programme for 2027, setting out its priorities for addressing cross-sectoral risks and strengthening coordination across the European Union's financial sector.

12 OCTOBER 2026

​The EBA releases the final technical package for release 4.4 of its reporting and Pillar 3 disclosure framework

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) today released the final technical package for version 4.4 of its reporting and disclosure framework, covering IFRS 18 reporting, Pillar 3 ESG disclosures and other technical amendments.

12 OCTOBER 2026

The EBA publishes Final Report on draft amendments to technical standards on prudential requirements for central securities depositories

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published a report proposing draft amendments to the Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) on certain prudential requirements for central securities depositories (CSDs) and designated credit institutions offering ‘banking-type ancillary services’. This mostly responds to changes introduced by the Central Securities Depositories Regulation (CSDR) “Refit”, notably those allowing banking CSDs to provide banking-type ancillary services to other CSDs.

9 OCTOBER 2026

EBA CRR3/CRD6 dashboard shows banks maintain capital levels well above minimum requirements under the fully loaded Basel III framework

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published its CRR3/CRD6 dashboard as of Q2 2026 for 129 banks at the highest level of consolidation across the EU/EEA. EU/EEA banks maintain capital levels well above minimum requirements under the fully loaded CRR3 framework, with an average Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio projected at 15.1%, despite an increased impact of the output floor compared with previous editions.

30 SEPTEMBER 2026

​The EBA publishes its 2027 Work Programme

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) published today its Work Programme outlining the key areas of focus and deliverables for 2027. The Programme also marks the first step towards a broader medium-term strategy to position the EBA as a next-generation regulator, a body developing supervision at new frontiers, and a provider of services to the supervisory and resolution community.

25 SEPTEMBER 2026

EU/EEA banks display strength amid a challenging risk environment

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published its Risk Dashboard for the second quarter of 2026. It summarises the main risks and vulnerabilities of the EU/EEA banking sector based on supervisory reporting data.

25 SEPTEMBER 2026

The EBA consults on revised technical standards for joint decisions on institution-specific capital and liquidity requirements

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today started a public consultation on proposed revisions to the Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) governing the joint decision process for institution-specific prudential requirements foreseen by Article 113 of the Capital Requirements Directive. The revisions are part of the EBA’s broader efforts to simplify and enhance the efficiency of the regulatory and supervisory framework, as outlined in its Report on the efficiency of the regulatory and supervisory framework, published in October 2025.

24 SEPTEMBER 2026

The EBA identifies priorities for the review of MiCA

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published its response to the European Commission’s targeted consultation on the review of the Regulation on Markets in Crypto-assets (MiCA). While MiCA has established a comprehensive regulatory framework for crypto-asset activities in the European Union, the crypto sector continues to evolve rapidly with new products, services and business models emerging to market. Against this backdrop, the EBA recommends the European Commission prioritise specific issues in its review of MiCA.

23 SEPTEMBER 2026

ESAs call for vigilance over external dependencies, cyber threats and private credit risks

The European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA and ESMA – the ESAs) have identified external dependencies, emerging technologies and private credit as key vulnerabilities for the EU financial system in their Autumn 2026 risk update.

18 SEPTEMBER 2026

The EBA publishes its final Guidelines on the management of third-party risk, delivering a more proportionate and consistent framework aligned with DORA

As part of the European Banking Authority’s (EBA) ongoing efforts to simplify its regulatory framework, the Guidelines focus on third-party arrangements supporting critical or important functions (CIFs) namely the disruption of which would materially impair the performance of a financial entity. By concentrating on these higher-risk arrangements, the Guidelines reduce unnecessary operational and supervisory burdens for less material ones while maintaining sound risk management.

16 SEPTEMBER 2026

​European Parliament confirms Thomas Gstädtner as Executive Director of the EBA

​Following a plenary vote in the European Parliament, Thomas Gstädtner has been confirmed as the new Executive Director of the European Banking Authority (EBA). Thomas Gstädtner, who will serve a five-year renewable term, was selected by the EBA Board of Supervisors from a shortlist of candidates following an open selection procedure.
