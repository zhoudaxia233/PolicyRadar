Governor Phil Scott Formally Proclaims Constitutional Amendment Proposals 3 and 4 Will Appear on November Ballot

Press Release
July 28, 2026

Montpelier, Vt. - Governor Phil Scott today formally notified Vermonters Proposals 3 and 4, proposed amendments to the Vermont Constitution passed by the General Assembly, will appear on the general election ballot in November. Pursuant to Section 72 of Chapter II of the Vermont Constitution and Chapter 32 of Title 17 of the Vermont Statutes Annotated, the Governor gave formal public notice of the proposed amendments by official proclamation.  

Proposal 3 proposes to codify in the Vermont Constitution employees’ right to collectively bargain with their employer and Proposal 4 would add an amendment to the Vermont Constitution guaranteeing people equal protection under the law.

 “Voting is one of the most important responsibilities we have as Americans,” said Governor Phil Scott. “I encourage voters to do their homework before making their voices heard on these issues in November.”

To amend Vermont’s Constitution, the state Senate must vote by a two-thirds majority to advance a proposal to the state House of Representatives. If the House concurs, by a simple majority, the proposal advances to the next biennial session of the General Assembly, where the House and Senate must both concur with the proposed amendment by simple majority. If passed, the proposal is brought before Vermonters by referendum. A majority of voters must approve of the measure for the constitution to be amended.
