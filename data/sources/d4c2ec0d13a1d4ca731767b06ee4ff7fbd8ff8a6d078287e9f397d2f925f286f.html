NC DNCR Announces Asia Opening Date for North Carolina Zoo

The North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources (DNCR) will open the North Carolina Zoo’s new Asia expansion on March 1, 2027. The 12.5-acre Asia region will feature innovative habitat designs and bring nearly 50 new species to the Zoo, including critically endangered Malayan tigers, Visayan warty pigs, white-cheeked gibbons, and Chinese alligators, among others.

About NC

From the Mountains to the Coast

A culture rich in history and arts, scenic beauty, a moderate climate, and world-class sports and recreational opportunities make North Carolina an exceptional place to live, visit and do business. Combine that with a skilled workforce, quality health care, top universities, a low cost of living, and it's easy to see why most people who live here never want to leave.
