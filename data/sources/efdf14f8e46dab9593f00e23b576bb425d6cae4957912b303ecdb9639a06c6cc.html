Acting Governor Scutari Signs Legislation

Posted on 10/8/2026

TRENTON – Today, Acting Governor Scutari signed the following bill into law: 

S-1395/A-4955 (Lagana, McKnight/Swain) – Allows certain limited liability companies to terminate alternate names before end of five-year registration period.
